About

The Story Behind the Lens

Filmmaker. Storyteller. Technologist. My journey began on the theatre stage and evolved through the film sets of the Indian film industry, eventually finding its way to the cutting edge of AI-driven visual creation.

As a Digital Imaging Technician, I understand the technical backbone of cinema. As an AI creator, I explore the future of visual storytelling. This unique combination allows me to craft content that respects traditional filmmaking while embracing the possibilities of artificial intelligence.

"Every project — whether a cinematic short, an AI commercial, or a YouTube reel is approached with the same philosophy: story first, technology as the enabler, never the focus."
